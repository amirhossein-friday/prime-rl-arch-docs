## Errors in other sections
- None contradicting. 06 §3.8/§7 already carries the world-size bug consistently.
- 05 says "cleanup deletes whole `step_N` incl `orchestrator/`". That holds only when `ckpt.output_dir` is unset. With a separate `ckpt.output_dir` the trainer tree has no `orchestrator/`, and orchestrator checkpoints under `<run_dir>/checkpoints` are never cleaned (R-B raised the same).
